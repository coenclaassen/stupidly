See stuff people ask.
Does it slow down my computer or internet?

It uses Chrome's built-in extension features and only wakes up when it has something to do. It's designed to be lightweight and it will not noticeably slow down your computer or internet.

Where does my data go?

It checks the sites you open to apply your limits. Your limits and usage stay locally in your browser. No ads, no analytics, nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I use it in Incognito?

Yes.

  1. Open chrome://extensions
  2. Click Details
  3. Toggle Allow in incognito.
What happens when I refresh?

Every refresh starts a new session. That stops the doom scroll. Your daily limit keeps counting, so you can't waste the whole day.

What happens when I hit my daily limit?

That site is done for the day. Come back tomorrow.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the extension icon and choose Remove from Chrome. Your feed will be delighted, but your scrolling might get back to eating your day.
