# Privacy

STUPIDLY checks the sites you open so it can apply the limits you set.

The extension stores your configured domains, session limits, daily limits, optional redirects, settings delay, and local usage time in Chrome storage on your device. This data is used only to enforce the limits you set.

- No ads.
- No analytics.
- No account.
- No remote code.
- Nothing is sold or sent to me or any third party.
- No human reads your browsing or usage data.

Browsing activity and usage time are used only for STUPIDLY’s single purpose: setting and enforcing website time limits. You can delete the stored data by removing the extension from Chrome.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/coenclaassen/stupidly/issues).
